Restoring the Old Salt Warehouse

The Brannock Quay salt warehouse reopened this spring after three years of restoration. Volunteers re-laid the oak floor, repaired the pulley beams and catalogued more than four hundred ledgers.

Photographs were contributed by members of the local history society, and the full archive is open to visitors on weekday afternoons.
