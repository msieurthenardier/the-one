## Decisions

- **Leg 1 mechanism: three-way merge of `allowlist.peers`** (Flight Director, 2026-11-02, leg design). The editor sends `allowlist_peers_base` (the peers of the record it last loaded or saved); the server keeps every stored peer not in the base and not in the operator's list. Rejected: a plain union (cannot remove a peer) and a stale-save 409 (needs a conflict UI, which is new operator-visible behaviour the mission forbids). The only visible change is the preserved peer appearing after Save, which the mission's constraint allows. No operator question needed under the flight's Design Decision ("with the operator if it changes what the editor shows").
