Notes on Growing Rhubarb

Rhubarb prefers a sunny spot with rich, well-drained soil. Divide crowns every five years in winter and never harvest in the first season after planting.

Photographs were contributed by members of the local history society, and the full archive is open to visitors on weekday afternoons.
