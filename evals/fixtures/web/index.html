Parish Noticeboard

Welcome to the parish noticeboard. This page gathers the documents that residents ask for most often, so that nobody has to hunt through the parish office for them. Each document is kept up to date by the volunteer who looks after it, and corrections are always welcome at the monthly meeting in the village hall.

Anyone planning a day by the water will want the Harrow Harbour tide chart, which is revised every month. Those travelling to the western isles should check the Skerra ferry timetable before setting out, since sailings change with the season.

Gardeners can join the queue for a plot by returning the Wick allotment waiting list form to the parish office. Walkers will find the Ember Fell trail map useful, and anyone organising an event can consult the Orrin community hall booking calendar to see which evenings are free.

The noticeboard is reviewed at the start of each quarter, and out-of-date notices are removed by the clerk.
