A Guide to Dry-Stone Walling

Dry-stone walls rely on well-chosen through-stones and a firm batter. Start with a trench, set the footings, and keep the hearting tight. A day's work is usually about two metres.

Photographs were contributed by members of the local history society, and the full archive is open to visitors on weekday afternoons.
