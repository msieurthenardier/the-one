# v0.11.1 — Agents know the date and time

Patch release over [v0.11.0](https://github.com/msieurthenardier/the-one/releases/tag/v0.11.0). Agents now know what day it is and can tell the time in the zone you choose.

## Added
- **Date and time page.** Settings › Date and time lets you follow the computer's time zone or choose one. It shows what agents will see and warns when your browser and the chosen zone disagree, with a one-click fix.
- **Agents know today's date.** Every conversation starts with the current date in your zone, in the app and in connector channels.
- **`current_time` tool.** Agents can ask for the current date, time and UTC offset in your zone or in another zone they name. It is part of every agent's starting tools and stays available to an agent whose tool list you trimmed. You can still turn it off for one agent on its Tools tab, or for all agents under Settings › Built-in tools, where it sits in a new "Time" category.
- Settings › Prompts shows both wordings of the date line.
- `the-one doctor` has a new check, `time zones`, which confirms the bundled time zone data is present.

## Changed
- Clock times, weekdays and day groups in the app (the chat list, message times, notes) now follow the time zone you chose.
- The README was rewritten to describe the app as a workbench for building, running and overseeing your own agents.

## Notes
- **Upgrading:** there are no schema changes and nothing to reconfigure. The zone setting starts on "follow the computer".
