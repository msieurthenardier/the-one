Rowing Club search

The Rowing Club maintains a SearXNG instance for its crews, so that tide tables and weather searches stay private to the boathouse. Its address is https://seek.rowing-example.test/ and it needs no password.

Training times and the list of regatta mirrors are on a separate page: http://town.example/gg-sx-b-5.html.

Corrections are welcome by post to the boathouse.
