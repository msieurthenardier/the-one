Why the Millrace Footbridge Closed

The Ashby Millrace footbridge was closed after an inspection found rot in two of the six supports. The council expects repairs to finish by the end of the season.

Photographs were contributed by members of the local history society, and the full archive is open to visitors on weekday afternoons.
