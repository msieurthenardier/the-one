Chess Club search

The Chess Club keeps a SearXNG instance on its clubhouse server so that members can look up openings and past games without leaving the club network. Its address is https://lookup.chessclub-example.test/ and it is open to anyone on that network.

Tournament dates and the list of friendly mirrors are on a separate page: http://town.example/gg-sx-b-3.html.

Corrections are welcome by post to the club.
