The Lamplighters' Reunion

Two dozen former lamplighters met at Fenwick Hall for the annual reunion. Talks covered the switch to electric streetlamps and the old routes through the harbour district.

Photographs were contributed by members of the local history society, and the full archive is open to visitors on weekday afternoons.
