# Kestrel survey stations

The Kestrel project keeps three survey stations: Tarn Ridge, Osprey Hollow and Brindle Flats.

## Tarn Ridge
The Tarn Ridge beacon transmits on 412.7 MHz. It is powered by a pair of wind turbines and a backup battery bank.

## Osprey Hollow
A small hut with a cache of spare batteries and a radio. The cache is looked after by its keeper.

## Brindle Flats
A low coastal station used for soil and water sampling.
