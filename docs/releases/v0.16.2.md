# v0.16.2 — Notes that keep up with you

Agents with notes on now clear out notes that have gone out of date, and some small local models remember more of what you mention in passing.

## Changed
- **Agents remove notes that are out of date.** If you mention that a plan has changed ("the trip is off"), an agent with notes on removes or updates the old note instead of keeping it.
- **Some small local models save more useful context.** When you mention a plan, preference or circumstance that may matter later ("I'm off to Lisbon next month"), some small models now save it far more often without being asked. In testing, LFM3 1.7B went from 3 in 20 conversations to 14 in 20. Claude agents already did this reliably.
- **Codes join the list of things never to save.** As well as passwords and keys, agents are now told not to save codes, such as a door code, in a note.

## Notes
- No database upgrade, and nothing needs to be reconfigured. Existing notes are kept as they are.
