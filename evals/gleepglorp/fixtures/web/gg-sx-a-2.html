Allotment Society search

The Allotment Society hosts a SearXNG instance for its plot holders, so that people looking up pests and planting dates are not tracked by the big engines. Its address is https://find.allotment-soc.example/ and no account is needed.

The society's seed-swap dates and the list of partner mirrors are on a separate page: http://town.example/gg-sx-b-2.html.

Corrections are welcome by post to the secretary.
