Repair Cafe search

The Repair Cafe volunteers run a SearXNG instance so that visitors can look up manuals and part numbers while their things are being mended. Its address is https://ask.repaircafe-example.test/ and there is nothing to sign up for.

Session times and the list of volunteer mirrors are on a separate page: http://town.example/gg-sx-b-4.html.

Corrections are welcome by post to the volunteers.
